Documents & Compliance

Export documents aligned before shipment.

The document set is prepared around the product, route, destination market, and importer requirements.

Trade documents

Documents should not become the weak point of the deal.

Mistakes in invoice, packing list, certificates, or product description can delay cargo release, complicate import, and create additional costs.

That is why the document framework is built in advance: before production, packaging, and batch handover to the carrier.

Basic set
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill

Compliance scope

Documents depend on the product, route, and importer requirements.

Certain categories may require additional certificates, declarations, or special forms.

01

Commercial documents

We align invoice, packing list, product description, package count, weight, volume, and delivery terms.

02

Origin

We check whether a Certificate of Origin is needed and whether the data is correct for the destination country.

03

Certificates

We clarify phytosanitary, quality, laboratory, or other documents required for the product category.

04

Shipping data

We synchronize documents with packaging, labeling, route, and carrier data.

Check documents

Tell us the product and import country.
