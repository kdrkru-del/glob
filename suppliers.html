Vietnam market entry

We are looking for manufacturers and suppliers ready to introduce their products to Vietnam.

GLOBERION GROUP welcomes proposals from companies interested in entering the Vietnamese market. We review the product, assess baseline import conditions, and help organize a targeted search for potential buyers, importers, distributors, and commercial partners in Vietnam.

Market-entry process

From product review to targeted buyer outreach.

The work is organized around product readiness, import feasibility, and a focused commercial approach to the Vietnamese market.

01

Product assessment

We review product specifications, origin, packaging, MOQ, pricing, production capacity, and the supplier’s export readiness.

02

Market review

We conduct a preliminary review of product-market fit, Vietnamese import requirements, documentation, labeling, and possible restrictions.

03

Offer preparation

We structure a clear offer for Vietnamese companies: description, specifications, pricing, supply terms, and presentation materials.

04

Buyer search

We conduct targeted outreach to importers, distributors, retail networks, industrial buyers, and other potential partners in Vietnam.

Suitable product directions

We consider products with a clear commercial proposition and realistic import pathway.

  • Food & agricultural products
  • Ingredients & raw materials
  • Timber, paper & packaging
  • Industrial raw materials
  • Equipment, components & tools
  • Consumer goods
  • Other categories after preliminary review

Buyer search does not guarantee a contract. The feasibility of promoting a product is determined after reviewing the product, documents, commercial terms, Vietnamese import requirements, and current market conditions.
