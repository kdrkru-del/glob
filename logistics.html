Shipment Planning

Logistics starts before the cargo is ready to ship.

The freight setup affects packaging, labeling, documents, production timing, and acceptance requirements. That is why logistics is involved before final order confirmation.

We match volume, urgency, budget, destination country, and supplier export readiness to select a realistic cargo movement setup.

Freight formats
  • FCL for container shipments
  • LCL for consolidated shipments
  • Air for urgent and light shipments
  • Multi-supplier consolidation
