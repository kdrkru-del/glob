Procurement Desk

When you need a local export desk in Vietnam.

Vietnam can be a strong sourcing market, but it requires careful checks: supplier, samples, production timing, documents, packaging, and export readiness should be aligned before payment.

GLOBERION GROUP works as an operating team on the deal side: collecting offers, comparing terms, helping verify suppliers, and keeping the process clear.

Use cases
  • Initial search for manufacturers and trading houses
  • Offer comparison by price, MOQ, and timing
  • Sample handling and specification lock-in
  • Batch control before carrier handover

Scope of work

We cover key stages before, during, and after the order.

Each project can be structured as a full cycle or as a separate module for a specific importer task.

01

Sourcing and shortlist

We identify relevant suppliers and clarify production capacity and initial commercial terms.

02

Commercial review

We compare price, timing, MOQ, payment, packaging, export readiness, and communication stability.

03

Samples and specification

We lock product parameters, packaging, labeling, and acceptance criteria before batch launch.

04

Execution control

We track production, document readiness, batch inspection, and cargo handover to logistics.
