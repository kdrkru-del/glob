Quality control

Quality control before export dispatch.

The control scope is set around supplier, specification, packaging, labeling, and batch risks.

Quality Assurance Framework

Control is needed beyond the inspection day.

If acceptance criteria are not fixed in advance, inspection becomes a dispute after production. That is why we start with supplier, sample, specification, and packaging checks.

The purpose of control is to reduce the risk of batch mismatch, delays, incorrect labeling, and documents that can affect import in the destination country.

What we check
  • Supplier and commercial readiness
  • Samples and acceptance criteria
  • Quantity, appearance, and packaging
  • Labeling and export readiness

Control stages

The check is built around the specific product and risk.

Not every project needs the same depth of control, so the scope is set for the deal.

01

Commercial filter

We compare offers and check how ready the supplier is for a real export deal.

02

Sample control

We lock product parameters, packaging, labeling, and acceptance criteria before batch production.

03

Batch inspection

We arrange checks for quantity, appearance, order compliance, and dispatch readiness.

04

Reporting

We provide a structured status on check results and questions to close.
